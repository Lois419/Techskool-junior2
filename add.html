Basic Operators (Addition & Subtraction)

Understand how to add and subtract numbers using place value.

Addition of Numbers

Addition involves combining numbers. Use place value positions like Units, Tens, Hundreds, and Thousands to align the digits correctly.

Example: Add 1425, 1231 and 30.

  • Add Units: 5 + 1 + 6
  • Add Tens: 2 + 3 + 3
  • Add Hundreds: 4 + 2 + 0
  • Add Thousands: 1 + 1 + 0

Total = 2,686

Subtraction of Numbers

Subtraction means taking away. You subtract digit by digit from right to left, just like in addition.

Example: Subtract 1,231 from 2,686:

  • Units: 6 - 1
  • Tens: 8 - 3
  • Hundreds: 6 - 2
  • Thousands: 2 - 1

Answer = 1,455

Word Problem Example

A man spent ₦2500 on housing, ₦1245 on savings, ₦3480 on feeding, and ₦248 on education. What is his total expenditure?

  • Units: 0 + 5 + 0 + 8
  • Tens: 0 + 4 + 8 + 4
  • Hundreds: 5 + 2 + 4 + 2
  • Thousands: 2 + 1 + 3 + 0

Total = ₦7,473
